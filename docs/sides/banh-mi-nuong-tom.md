# Bánh Mì Nướng Tôm (Vietnamese Shrimp Toast)

*Yield: ~36–40 pieces (about 2 baguettes' worth) · Active 20 min · Total 35 min*

Baked baguette slices topped with a creamy shrimp-and-crab paste. A party-platter staple —
Vietnamese in seasoning, Cantonese in ancestry, with a Korean chili edge in this version.

## Ingredients

**Seafood base**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Raw shrimp, peeled & deveined | 1 lb / 450 g |
| Imitation crab | 8 oz / 225 g |

**Binder**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Mayonnaise (Kewpie preferred) | ⅓ cup / 80 g |
| Low-moisture mozzarella, shredded | 1 cup / 113 g, plus ¼ cup extra for topping |
| Unsalted butter, melted | 1 tbsp *(optional)* |

**Seasoning**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fish sauce | 1 tbsp |
| Kosher salt | ½ tsp |
| Granulated sugar | ½ tsp |
| White pepper | ½ tsp |
| Toasted sesame oil | 2 tsp |
| Sriracha | 1–2 tbsp |
| Gochugaru (Korean red pepper flakes) | 1–2 tsp |

**Aromatics**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Green onion, finely chopped | 4–5 stalks (~½ cup), reserve some green tops for garnish |
| Garlic, minced | 4 cloves (~1 tbsp) |
| Celery, finely diced | ¼ cup *(optional)* |

**Bread**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Baguettes | 2 loaves |

## Procedure

1. Pat the shrimp thoroughly dry. Chop by hand to a rough mince — pea-sized pieces, not a
   purée. If using a food processor, pulse in short bursts and stop while the texture is still
   uneven; over-processing gives a rubbery, pâté-like result.
2. Shred or roughly chop the imitation crab into similar-sized pieces.
3. Combine the shrimp, crab, mayonnaise, 1 cup mozzarella, butter, fish sauce, salt, sugar,
   white pepper, sesame oil, sriracha, gochugaru, green onion, garlic, and celery — reserving
   the ¼ cup extra cheese and some green onion tops for garnish. Mix thoroughly until it forms
   a cohesive, scoopable paste that holds a mound on a spoon. If it slumps, add cheese a
   tablespoon at a time; if it's stiff and crumbly, add mayo a tablespoon at a time.
4. Pan-fry a teaspoon of the mixture for 60 seconds and taste it. Adjust salt/fish sauce before
   committing the rest of the batch.
5. Rest the paste 20 minutes (or overnight) — the salt firms up the shrimp proteins and the
   mixture binds noticeably better.
6. Slice baguettes ½–¾ inch thick, straight down for bite-sized pieces or on a diagonal for
   larger ones.
7. Toast the bare slices at 350 °F / 175 °C for 4–5 minutes until dry to the touch. This is
   the single biggest defence against a soggy base, and essential if assembling ahead.
8. Spread about 2 tbsp of paste per slice, edge to edge, roughly ¼ inch thick. Press down
   gently so it adheres. Sprinkle the reserved cheese over the tops.
9. Bake at 350 °F / 175 °C for 12–14 minutes, until the cheese is golden and bubbling and the
   shrimp is opaque and set.
10. Move to the top rack and broil 2–3 minutes for a golden, blistered surface — watch it
    continuously at this stage.
11. Garnish with reserved green onion. Serve immediately; the crust softens within about 20
    minutes.

**Notes**

- **Shrimp:** 31/40 count is fine; pat very dry before chopping.
- **Imitation crab** is already salted — factor that in before adding extra salt.
- **Mayonnaise:** up to ½ cup for a looser, creamier spread.
- **Cheese choice.** Mozzarella alone is stretchy but browns poorly and sheds water. A Mexican
  blend, mild cheddar, or colby-jack browns better. Half mozzarella / half cheddar is a good
  compromise.
- **Sesame oil** is potent — resist going higher than 2 tsp.
- **Salt:** reduce the ½ tsp kosher salt if the crab is very salty.
- **Fresh crab:** substitute 1:1 for imitation crab, but drop the sugar and add salt at the
  end — real crab is far less sweet and less salty.
- **Make-ahead:** paste keeps 1 day refrigerated; assembled toasts hold 8 h before baking.
- **Air fryer:** 375 °F / 190 °C for 10–12 minutes, no pre-toast needed.
- **Pan-fried version** (bánh mì chiên tôm): spread on untoasted slices and fry paste-side down
  in ½ inch of oil, 3–4 minutes, then flip briefly.
- **Freezing:** freeze assembled, unbaked toasts flat on a tray, then bag. Bake from frozen at
  400 °F / 205 °C for 18–20 minutes.
- **Scaling ratios**, anchored to shrimp weight: imitation crab ≈ 50% of shrimp weight; cheese
  ≈ 25%; mayo ≈ 18% (adjust to consistency, not to the number); ~2 tbsp paste per slice; about
  1 baguette per 1¼ lb of paste.
